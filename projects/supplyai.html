Supplychain AI — an internal copilot for ops teams.

An exploratory prototype: what if supply-chain coordinators had a structured AI copilot that surfaced exceptions, drafted comms, and never made decisions on its own?

RoleSolo product designer · AI workflow design · interaction patterns.
TypeInternal prototype · exploratory work.
UsersSupply-chain coordinators, planners, ops managers.
StackLLM-backed surface · structured JSON intents · mock data.
01 · The premise

Ops doesn't need a chatbot. It needs a second pair of eyes.

Supply-chain coordinators don't want to chat — they want exceptions surfaced and the boring writing done. The prototype focuses on three loops: identify late shipments, draft customer communications, propose mitigation steps. The model never sends anything — humans approve, every time.

02 · The pattern

Same translator pattern. Different domain.

Echoes The Translator Pattern: LLM understands intent and reads the data, deterministic logic enforces the action. Coordinators get summaries they can act on, not advice they have to argue with.

03 · Highlights

Key moves.

01

Exceptions, not chat

The UI surfaces problems with one-click drill-downs — no chat-first interface.

02

Drafted comms

Customer emails drafted, never sent. Coordinator approves or rejects every send.

03

Audit log first

Every AI action logged. Reviewable. Reversible.
